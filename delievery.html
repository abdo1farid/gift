My baby,

I didn't want this day to pass without saying it properly, so I wrote it all down. Six months ago I didn't know how much one person could change the way ordinary days feel. Now I can't picture a day without you in it.

I made you this small gift hopeing that you will like it, and just to tell you exactly that you are the reason that I finally recognised what falling in love actually looks like. It's been every bit as real as I hoped it would be, and you're the reason it is.

Your love is my drug, and I'm not even embarrassed to say it. Some days I catch myself thinking about you in the middle of something completely unrelated, and I smile like an idiot. And people around would ask me questions like who made you smile wide like that, and I'll tell them my Kaylee, cuz trully you do/ I hope you know that every time you laugh, or look over like I'm the only one that matters, it lands somewhere deep in me.

When I found you, I stopped looking. That's the simplest way I can put it. I'm so glad I found you, my love. Out of everyone who could have been on the other side of the conversation, it was you, and I still feel lucky about that.

I miss you constantly. Even when we're together, there's a small part of me that's already counting the hours until the next time, until the day we can meet in real life. I don't think that will ever stop, and honestly I don't want it to.

There's also a pull between us that I can't quite explain. It's in the small things: how we finish each other's thoughts, how quiet can feel comfortable with you, how I always seem to find my way back to you. It feels like something we're building together without even trying.

What I hope for the next six months, and for everything after them, is simple. I want more mornings like the ones we've already had, more late-night talks, more plans that we make and actually keep. I want to keep learning you, and I want you to keep choosing me, the same way I'm choosing you.

I wish we always stay forever, my baby. I know it's a big thing to say, but I mean it with my whole heart. Thank you for every single day so far. Here's to the next six months, and to all the ones after that.

Forever yours,
